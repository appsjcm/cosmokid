Astronauta spritesheet para Unity/Godot
Canvas: 1536x1536
Grid: 8 columnas x 6 filas
Celda: 192x256px

IDLE: fila 0, 4 frames
RUN: fila 1, 8 frames
JUMP: fila 2, 5 frames
FALL: fila 3, 4 frames
ATTACK: fila 4, 6 frames
HURT: fila 5, 3 frames

Orden de filas:
0 idle
1 run
2 jump
3 fall
4 attack
5 hurt

Importar en Unity: Sprite Mode = Multiple, Slice = Grid by Cell Size, Cell Size = 192x256.
Importar en Godot: HFrames = 8, VFrames = 6, usar solo los frames necesarios de cada fila.
